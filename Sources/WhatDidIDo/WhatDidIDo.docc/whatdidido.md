# ``whatdidido``

A pretty wrapper for your shell history. Answers the questions you actually ask yourself at the terminal.

## Overview

`whatdidido` surfaces your shell history in the way you actually think about it — ranked, filtered, highlighted, and ready to act on. Instead of scrolling through a wall of raw history or wrestling with `history | grep`, you get purpose-built commands for every common question:

```bash
whatdidido recent        # What did I just do?
whatdidido top           # What do I type most?
whatdidido for git       # How do I use git again?
whatdidido summary       # What was I working on?
```

Shell and OS are auto-detected from your environment — you rarely need to pass anything extra.
